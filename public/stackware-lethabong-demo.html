What we do

From dry ground to running water

Everything you need to find, reach and use groundwater — under one team.

Borehole Drilling

Percussion and rotary drilling to any required depth, on residential, farm and commercial sites.

Pump Installation

Submersible and pressure pumps sized to your borehole yield and household or irrigation demand.

Water Tanks

Storage tanks and stands supplied and installed, so you have water on tap even when the power's out.

Borehole Flushing

Clearing and developing existing boreholes to restore flow and remove silt build-up.

Filtration Systems

Sediment and iron filtration to protect your pump and appliances from grit and staining.

Water Purification

Testing and purification so your borehole water is safe to drink, cook and wash with.
