About Me

I'm a Senior Developer and Full-Stack Engineer based in Pretoria, South Africa, with a strong foundation in Information Technology. My journey began with a fascination for how software systems are built end-to-end — from intuitive user interfaces to robust, scalable back-end architectures that power real-world products.

Over the years, I have deepened my expertise in ASP.NET Core, C#, Entity Framework Core, Azure, and MVC architecture. I specialise in building marketplace platforms, admin dashboards, and data-driven systems that solve real problems — including architecting the ShelfTextbooks student marketplace with escrow payments and analytics, and co-building UnganaConnect as both Business Analyst and Frontend Developer using React.

My philosophy is simple: write clean, maintainable code, design with the user in mind, and build systems that can grow over time. Great software isn't just functional — it's thoughtful, accessible, and built to last.
