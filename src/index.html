How It Works

From First Call to Handover

You always know what's happening and what happens next.

Discovery

A short call to confirm your brand direction, product range, how you deliver and how you want to take payment. You get a fixed quote after this — not before.

Build

Theme setup, brand styling, products and collections, payments, shipping, policies and pages. You see progress as it happens, not only at the end.

Testing

A full test order on desktop and mobile before anything goes live: add to cart, checkout, payment, confirmation email, and the order landing correctly in admin.

Launch

Domain connected, SSL verified, store opened. I stay close through the first orders in case anything needs adjusting.

Handover

Training so your team can add products, run promotions and manage orders without me. The store stays in your name and you are never locked in.

About Me

I run Nsovo Systems. Nsovo means mercy in Tsonga — and it's the standard I hold the work to: built with care, handed over clean, and never locked to me.

I'm a full-stack developer in Pretoria, and e-commerce is where most of my work starts — the whole store, not just the storefront: product catalogues and variants, cart and checkout, payment handling, order and delivery workflows, and the admin dashboard the owner actually runs the business from. From there I also build web apps, business systems, and the hosting and support behind them.

I've built online stores from scratch rather than only assembling them from templates — a marketplace with escrow payments, refunds and dispute resolution; a bakery store with live order pricing, revenue analytics and automated receipts; and a payments system handling SWIFT transactions with multi-factor authentication and encryption. That means when something on your store needs to behave a particular way, I can make it.

How I work is simple: agree the scope up front, build it, test a real order end to end before launch, then train you to run it yourself. The work stays in your name and you're never locked into me. Behind Nsovo Systems it's just me, Britney Chauke — so you always deal with the person doing the work. I also take on full-time roles; my CV is below.
